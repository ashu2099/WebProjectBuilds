Why Choose Polygreens

Unmatched Durability. Unwavering Trust.

Virgin (Non-Recycled) Polymer

  • Crafted exclusively from pure, fresh high-density polyethylene (HDPE), with zero recycled content.
  • This ensures superior strength, consistency, and maximum tear resistance.
  • Guarantees a longer service life and better return on investment compared to recycled alternatives.

Advanced UV Protection

  • Features premium-grade UV stabilizers integrated directly into the polymer weave.
  • Actively resists photodegradation, preventing the net from becoming brittle or cracking prematurely.
  • Ensures the shadenet maintains its precise shading factor and structural integrity for years.

Optimized Dust Management

  • Due to the virgin raw materials used, our product has a consistent, smooth fibre structure which creates an effective mesh.
  • This optimization allows for better capture and filtration of airborne dust, pollen, and debris.
  • The high-quality surface is easier to maintain and clean, ensuring lasting clarity.
